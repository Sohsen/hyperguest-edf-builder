Annotations
 
Accommodation taxes are taxes and fees that are applied to an accommodation.
        The accommodation tax EDF specifies rule-based tax information.
        The XML format is derived from the Hotel global extra EDF structure and generally uses the same logic.
        Accommodation tax files are delivered per supplier, hotel or contract.

        Examples for accommodation taxes and fees:
        • general sales tax/value added tax (GST/VAT)
        • city tax
        • lodging tax
        • transfer fee
        • resort fee
        • destination marketing fee
        • tourism fee

        The structure of a global accommodation tax file is roughly as follows:

        AccommodationTaxesRoot
        //AccommodationIdentifier
        //Currency
        //Taxes >> Tax (1 .. n)

Annotations
 
The Sequence attribute describes a standard stay that the product can be booked with.

Any stay duration (up to any maximum stay limit set) which can be divided by the Sequence with a remainder of 0 is possible.

The value of the Sequence attribute must be less than 63.

Example:
Sequence="P7D"
The product can be booked by week up to the maximum stay duration, where set.

Example:
Max="P28D" Sequence="P7D"
This product can be booked with a stay of 7, 14, 21 and 28 days.

Example:
Min="P5D" Sequence="P5D"
The product can be booked with a minimum stay of 5 days, and all further stays are bookable with the factor 5.

Example:
Max="P18D" Sequence="P5D"
The product can be booked  with a stay of 5, 10 and 15 days.

<Restriction>
<DateRestrictions>
