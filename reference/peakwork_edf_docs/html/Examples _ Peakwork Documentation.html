Rule and calculation﻿

<Rules>
    <Rule Name="rule_1" CalculationName="calc_1">
        <Distribution>
            <Channel Name="dc_1"/>
        </Distribution>
    </Rule>
</Rules>
<Calculations>
    <Calculation Name="calc_1" Mode="AbsoluteRelative" ApplyTo="HotelPrice">
        <Charges>
            <Charge StayMode="Hotel" PersonMode="PerPerson">
                <Range Min="0" Max="999999999"/>
                <Pricing Percent="10"/>
            </Charge>
        </Charges>
    </Calculation>
</Calculations>

The CalculationName attribute of a Rule element (line 2) refers to a Calculation element (line 9).

If the conditions in the Rule element are met, we apply the calculation named calc_1.

If the price is between 0 and 999999999, the calculation adds 10% to the price.

        <Charge Id="1" PersonMode="PerPerson">

        </Charge>

            <Range Min="600.00000" Max="699.99000"/>

            <Range Min="700.00000" Max="799.99000"/>

        <Charge Id="5" PersonMode="PerPerson">
            <Range Min="900.00000" Max="999.99000"/>
            <Pricing Amount="1.00000" Percent="15.00000"/>

        <Charge Id="6" PersonMode="PerPerson">
            <Range Min="1000.00000"/>
            <Pricing Amount="1.00000" Percent="14.00000"/>
        </Charge>
    </Charges>
</Calculation>

If the price is between 0 and 599.99, the calculation adds 1€ and 19% to the price.

If the price is between 600.00 and 699.99, the calculation adds 1€ and 18% to the price.

And so on...

Fixed prices (Mode="Fix")﻿

<Calculation Name="calc_fix" Mode="Fix">
    <Charges>
        <Charge PersonMode="PerPerson">
            <Range Min="1.00000" Max="198.00000"/>
            <Pricing Amount="198.00000"/>
        </Charge>
    </Charges>
</Calculation>

If the price is between 1.00 and 198.00, the calculation sets the price to 198.00.

Relative and absolute changes (Mode="AbsoluteRelative")﻿

<Calculation Name="calc_absrel" Mode="AbsoluteRelative">
    <Charges>
        <Charge PersonMode="PerPerson">
            <Range Min="1.00000" Max="198.00000"/>
            <Pricing Amount="198.00000" Percent="10"/>
        </Charge>
    </Charges>
</Calculation>

If the price is between 1.00 and 198.00, the calculation first adds 10% to the price and after that increases the price by 198.00.

Absolute and relative changes (Mode="RelativeAbsolute")﻿

<Calculation Name="calc_relabs" Mode="RelativeAbsolute">
    <Charges>
        <Charge PersonMode="PerPerson">
            <Range Min="1.00000" Max="198.00000"/>
            <Pricing Amount="198.00000" Amount="10"/>
        </Charge>
    </Charges>
</Calculation>

If the price is between 1.00 and 198.00, the calculation first adds 198.00 to the price and after that increases the price by 10%.

Multiple restrictions﻿

<Rule Name="rule_h123" SortIndex="1" CalculationName="144">
    <AirportRestrictions>
        <DepartureAirports>
            <DepartureAirport IataCode="ACE"/>
            <DepartureAirport IataCode="ZAZ"/>
        </DepartureAirports>
        <ArrivalAirports>
            <ArrivalAirport IataCode="SXF"/>
            <ArrivalAirport IataCode="TXL"/>
        </ArrivalAirports>
    </AirportRestrictions>
    <DateRestrictions>
        <DateRestriction ApplyTo="Arrival" ApplyToMode="Flight"
                Start="2017-06-01" End="2017-06-30"
                BookingStart="2017-04-01" BookingEnd="2017-04-30"/>
    </DateRestrictions>
    <StayRestrictions>
        <StayRestriction Min="P2D" Max="P3D" Mode="Hotel"/>
    </StayRestrictions>
    <HotelRestrictions>
        <HotelRestriction>
            <Hotels>
                <Hotel Code="123"/>
            </Hotels>
        </HotelRestriction>
    </HotelRestrictions>
    <GuestRestrictions Operator="AND">
        <GuestRestriction MinPerson="2" MaxPerson="2" PersonType="A"/>
        <GuestRestriction MinPerson="0" MaxPerson="0" PersonType="C"/>
        <GuestRestriction MinPerson="0" MaxPerson="9" PersonType="I"/>
    </GuestRestrictions>
    <PriceRestrictions>
        <PriceRestriction MinPrice="1.0" MaxPrice="284.0" ApplyTo="TotalPrice"/>
    </PriceRestrictions>
</Rule>

Multiple restrictions are joined by a logical AND. All conditions must be met:

  • departure at ACE or ZAZ

  • arrival at SXF or TXL

  • arrival is in June and booking is in April and length of stay is 2 or 3 days

  • hotel has the code 123

  • travel group consists of two adults and up to 9 infants

  • offer has a net price between 1.0 and 284.0
